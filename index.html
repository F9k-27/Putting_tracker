Welcome to discgolf putting tracker

Track your putting accuracy across distances (1–35 m / 3–115 ft).

  • Log putts: Pick your distance, set how many discs are in your bag, log how many you missed, then press OK to save your success rate for that distance.
  • Slider: The slider only goes up to 20 m — use the arrow buttons for 21–35 m.
  • 🧍 Stance: Toggle to pick your putting stance.
  • 🎯 Accuracy: Arm it to mark where each disc lands after OK.
  • 🎮 Game: Random distance every throw — hit Sink or Miss.
  • End Practice: Session summary, stats and landing heatmaps.
  • Data: Download / upload sessions as JSON.
  • Support: Back the project via PayPal.
