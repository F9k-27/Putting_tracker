# Putting Tracker

![Putting Tracker preview](images/Putting_tracker.png)

## About Putting Tracker

This tool helps you track your putting accuracy across different distances.

Please share it with your fellow discgolfers! We are working on new features and improvements.
[Link](https://f9k-27.github.io/Putting_tracker/) to the application.

## Features

- **Distance tracking** — Pick any distance from 1–35 m (3–115 ft) with the slider or arrow buttons, choose how many discs are in your bag, log your misses, and press **OK** to record your success rate for that distance. Meters/feet toggle in the top-right.
- **Stance selector** — Tap the **🧍 Stance** toggle to reveal a picker for Staggered, Straddle, Kneeling, Turbo, Split, and Lunge putts. **Jump putt** unlocks only when the distance is greater than 10 m.
- **Accuracy practice (🎯)** — Arm the crosshair button next to the game button, then after pressing **OK** a pop-up basket appears where you tap to mark where each disc landed (one tap per disc). Landing marks are stored per distance for the current session.
- **Game mode (🎮)** — The distance changes randomly every throw. Set the min/max distance and total throws, hit **Sink** or **Miss** for each, and see your final score and success rate. Optionally have the **stance change randomly** each throw. The accuracy toggle is hidden while a game is running.
- **End Practice summary** — Click **End Practice** for a session overview: total putts, makes/misses, overall success rate, distances practiced, sessions, and your best & toughest distances.
- **Landing heatmaps** — If you recorded accuracy marks, the summary shows a heatmap of where your discs landed (with your average spot per distance), plus a **per-distance** view with individual heatmaps, a consistency score, and horizontal/vertical grouping bias.
- **Import / export** — Download your stats as JSON or upload a previous session. (Accuracy heatmap marks are session-only and are not part of the export.)
